The method

No indicators. Just price, structure and patience.

Over a decade of reading charts the same way, across FX, crypto, indices and commodities. This is how every video, every live session and every post is built.

Map the structure

Start from the higher timeframes. Where has price turned before? Where is liquidity resting? Where will the week most likely be decided? Levels are drawn before the week opens, not after.

Wait for price to speak

A level is not a trade. A setup only exists once price confirms it at the level, with a trigger you can name. The videos show the ones taken and the ones skipped, and why.

Manage, don't hope

Partial exits, stops that move for a reason, and the discipline to leave a working trade alone. Management is shown live in the private feed as it happens.

Journal every trade

Entries, exits, mistakes and lessons, on video, during the week. Reviewing is where the learning actually happens, for members and for us.

Why pure price action

Indicators are derived from price, with a delay. Reading price directly removes the delay and the false confidence. One chart, one language, every market.
